Neural Origins & Theory

Explore the historical foundations of Artificial Intelligence and test your knowledge of McCulloch-Pitts Neurons.

Timeline of AI Origins

1943: The First Mathematical Model

Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts

They published "A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity", introducing the first simplified mathematical model of a biological neuron (the MCP Neuron), using binary inputs and threshold logic.

1957: The Perceptron

Frank Rosenblatt

Building on MCP, Rosenblatt created the Perceptron, introducing learnable weights via a training algorithm, allowing the machine to learn from examples instead of hard-coding the weights.

Modern Deep Learning

Today

Using millions of artificial neurons arranged in deep layers (Hidden Layers) with non-linear activation functions (ReLU, Sigmoid), powering ChatGPT, self-driving cars, and AlphaFold.

Knowledge Flashcards

What is a Threshold (θ)?

The minimum weighted sum (z) required for the neuron to fire (output 1). If z < θ, output is 0.

Biggest limitation of MCP?

It cannot learn. Weights and thresholds must be manually set by an engineer. Also, a single node cannot solve non-linear problems like XOR.
